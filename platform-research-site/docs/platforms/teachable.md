!!! info "Great Courses, Incomplete Platform"
    Best-in-class course delivery and student UX (8/10), native video hosting with mobile apps, and automatic tax handling. But no CRM, no email marketing, and no landing pages — you'd need 2-3 external tools.

**Recommended:** Builder ($69/mo annual) — 0% transaction fee, 10 products.

!!! warning "Starter Plan Trap"
    The 7.5% transaction fee on Starter is painful. At $1,500/month revenue, you'd pay $112.50/month in platform fees alone — more than the Builder plan costs.

## Core Features

### Course Builder — 8/10

!!! success "Tied for Best Course Builder"
    Block-based curriculum editor with native video hosting (up to 1TB), drip scheduling, certificates, quizzes, and native iOS/Android student apps on all plans.

- **Video hosting:** Up to 1TB (2TB on Advanced), unlimited upload
- **Drip content:** Timed scheduling, perfect for 30-day program
- **Progress tracking:** Per-student completion, compliance features
- **Certificates:** Customizable completion certificates (all plans)
- **Quizzes:** Auto-graded + short answer assessments
- **Coaching:** Built-in 1-on-1 session scheduling product type
- **Community:** Built-in (newer feature, maturing)
- **Mobile apps:** **Native iOS & Android** on all plans
- **AI:** AI-assisted course creation
- **Subtitles/translations:** Free on all plans

### Landing Page Builder — 4/10

- Per-course sales page editor only
- No freeform drag-and-drop, no A/B testing
- No standalone landing pages, no pop-ups
- Cannot build a full marketing website

### Lead Capture & CRM — 2/10

- Student management only (enrolled list)
- Abandoned cart emails (automated)
- **No CRM, no contact records, no pipeline**
- **No opt-in forms** for non-students
- **No email sequences** beyond basic notifications
- **No SMS**

### E-Commerce — 8/10

- teachable:pay handles everything including **automatic tax compliance** (US sales tax, global VAT, GST)
- Upsells & order bumps (all plans)
- Cart recovery / abandoned cart emails
- Coupons, bundles, payment plans
- Built-in affiliate program
- 130+ currencies, daily payouts
