## Core Features

### Course Builder — 6/10

- "Classroom" tab within each group
- Modules > Lessons with video, text, file attachments
- Videos hosted on Skool directly
- **Drip content:** Yes, by join date or calendar dates
- **Progress tracking:** Mark complete, progress bars. Limited per-member analytics.
- **Quizzes:** No
- **Certificates:** No
- **Mobile:** Good native iOS/Android apps

### Landing Page Builder — 2/10

!!! danger "Critical Gap"
    Skool has **no landing page builder**. Each group gets one fixed-format "About" page at skool.com/your-group. No customization, no sales videos, no countdowns, no A/B testing.

### Lead Capture & CRM — 2/10

- No form builder, no opt-in forms, no lead magnets
- **No email marketing whatsoever**
- No automation builder, no tagging, no workflows, no segmentation

### E-Commerce — 3/10

- Subscription billing only (monthly/annual via Stripe)
- **Cannot sell one-time products** — a $14.99 ebook is impossible on Skool
- No upsells, no order bumps, no product catalog
- Stripe only (no PayPal)

## What Makes Skool Special

!!! success "Best-in-Class Community Engagement"
    Points-based leaderboard where members earn points for posting, commenting, completing courses. Customizable unlock levels (Level 3 = bonus module). Drives genuine engagement — ideal for transformation programs.

## Migration Assessment

- **Custom Domain:** **Not available.** URL is always skool.com/your-group.
- **Time-to-Live:** Group + basic course: 1-3 days. External tools: 1-2 days. Total: 2-3 weeks.
- **Exit Strategy:** Member list (CSV). Community posts NOT exportable. **Biggest lock-in risk is community content.**
