## Core Features

### Course Builder — 8/10 { .course-rating }

!!! success "Highest Priority Feature — Strong"
    Kajabi's course builder is one of the best in the all-in-one category, rivaling dedicated LMS platforms.

- Structure: Products > Modules > Lessons
- Content types: video, text, PDF, audio, quizzes
- **"Cohort Courses"** on Growth+ for time-boxed group programs (ideal for 30-day program)
- **Video hosting:** Native (Wistia infrastructure). Adaptive bitrate streaming. Upload directly. Transcriptions/translations on Pro.
- **Drip content:** Full support. Schedule by enrollment date or calendar dates. Cohort mode for synchronized starts.
- **Progress tracking:** Built-in completion tracking. Progress bars. Admin per-student dashboard.
- **Certificates:** Auto-generated, customizable, triggered on completion.
- **Quizzes:** Multiple choice, true/false. Pass/fail gates possible.
- **Student experience:** Clean modern member portal. Kajabi mobile app (iOS/Android). Branded app on Pro.
- **Community:** Built-in feed-based with posts, comments, challenges.

### Landing Page Builder — 6/10

- Section-based drag-and-drop (not pixel-level freeform)
- 20-30+ templates by use case
- Mobile-responsive with auto SSL
- **No native A/B testing** — notable gap
- Limited creative freedom vs dedicated builders

### Lead Capture & CRM — 7/10

- Built-in opt-in forms (inline, popup)
- **Unlimited marketing emails** on all plans
- Visual email builder + automated sequences
- Trigger-based automations (purchase, form, tag, lesson complete)
- Tag-based segmentation
- **Not a traditional CRM**: no pipeline stages, no deal management

### E-Commerce — 8/10

- Courses, coaching, memberships, downloads, podcasts, newsletters
- One-time, subscriptions, payment plans, 140+ currencies
- Order bumps, post-purchase upsells/downsells
- Promo codes
- Built-in affiliate management on all plans
- Apple Pay / Google Pay via Kajabi Payments

## Migration Assessment

- **Custom Domain:** CNAME/A-record. SSL auto-provisioned. 1-24h propagation.
- **Content Import:** No automated course import. Videos re-uploaded individually. Contacts via CSV. Budget 2-4 hours for 30 videos.
- **Time-to-Live:** Book sales page: 1-2 days. Full 30-day course: 7-14 days. Complete migration: **2-4 weeks.**
- **Exit Strategy:** Contacts exportable. Videos must be downloaded individually. Pages/sequences not exportable. **Lock-in risk: Moderate-High.** Plan 2-4 weeks if leaving.
