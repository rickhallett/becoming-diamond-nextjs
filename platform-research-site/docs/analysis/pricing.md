# Pricing Comparison

What does each platform actually cost for a solo transformation coach running: 1 landing page, lead capture, $14.99 book sales, and a 30-day video course with drip?

## Monthly Cost (Recommended Tier)

| Platform | Tier | Monthly | Annual (per mo) | Tx Fee | Extra Costs | **True Monthly** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Systeme.io** | Startup | $27 | $19 | 0% | Stripe only | **$19-27** |
| **GHL** | Starter | $97 | $79 | 0% | SMS/email ~$10-30 | **$89-127** |
| **Kartra** | Starter | $119 | $99 | 0% | Stripe only | **$99-119** |
| **Teachable** | Builder | $89 | $69 | 0% | + email ~$29 + pages | **$98-118** |
| **Kajabi** | Basic | $179 | $143 | 2.9%+$0.30 | None | **$143-179** |
| **Skool** | Single | $99 | $99 | 0% | + email + pages + book tool | **$147-167** |
| **WordPress** | Full stack | ~$117 | ~$93 | 0% | + developer $100-300 | **$193-393** |
| **ClickFunnels** | Pro | $297 | $248 | 0% | + community ~$20-50 | **$268-347** |

## Transaction Fee Impact

On $14.99 book sales, assuming 100 sales/month ($1,499 revenue):

| Platform | Platform Fee | Stripe Fee | Total Fees | Net Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Systeme.io | $0 | $73.47 | $73.47 | $1,425.53 |
| GHL | $0 | $73.47 | $73.47 | $1,425.53 |
| Kartra | $0 | $73.47 | $73.47 | $1,425.53 |
| ClickFunnels | $0 | $73.47 | $73.47 | $1,425.53 |
| Kajabi (native) | $0 | $73.47 | $73.47 | $1,425.53 |
| Kajabi (Stripe, Basic) | $29.98 (2%) | $73.47 | $103.45 | $1,395.55 |
| Teachable (Builder) | $0 | $73.47 | $73.47 | $1,425.53 |
| Teachable (Starter) | **$112.43 (7.5%)** | $73.47 | **$185.90** | $1,313.10 |

!!! note
    At 100 sales/month, transaction fees are roughly equivalent across 0% platforms. The difference is in platform subscription cost. Teachable Starter's 7.5% fee makes it more expensive than Builder at this volume.
