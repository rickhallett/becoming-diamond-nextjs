# Conscious Choices

If you don't change the script, the ending stays the same.

{{video:3ab2c7a1-0b46-41a8-91ad-d55ed93781a4}}

You'll discover the power of making conscious decisions from your heart rather than just your head. While logic has its place, your heart is an intelligence center where your authentic "diamond self" lives—capable of guiding you beyond your comfort zone into genuine transformation.

Your practice today involves identifying three choices you need to make, analyzing each logically, then using heart-focused breathing to feel what your heart says. You'll then take action on one decision immediately.

Once you decide, you'll commit fully without second-guessing. Real change begins with conscious choice, and acting creates the liberating energy that moves your life forward.
