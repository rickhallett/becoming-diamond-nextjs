# The First Blade: The Body Check

Your body is the first blade of the Swiss Army Knife.

{{video:2bf807d4-37a8-4365-a1cb-3dd47a0161d7}}

Day 2 focuses on your first transformation tool: body language. You'll discover how changing your posture shifts your mood, hormones, and mindset, training your nervous system to rise under pressure instead of folding.

You'll practice three daily actions: a 2-minute Power Pose (hands on hips or raised in a V), Conscious Posture check-ins throughout the day, and observing how others carry themselves. 

Each time you stand tall instead of collapsing, you're creating a new pattern of strength. You'll learn that posture equals presence—a fundamental tool for standing strong in any situation.
