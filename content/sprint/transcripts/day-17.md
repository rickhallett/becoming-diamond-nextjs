## Recap: What's In Your Cup

Welcome to Day 17, Broadcasting Begins. Yesterday on Day 16, we talked about what's in your cup and how life bumps you and whatever is inside of you spills out. Today, we flip it. Now the question is, what emotion am I activating in my body and what signal am I transmitting to the world?

## Understanding Broadcasting

Broadcasting does take effort, but not the kind of effort that most people think. Broadcasting is simply your ability to *shift your frequency*. Another term for this is *emotional regulation*. 

And here's the part that most people miss: *you are always broadcasting*. It takes zero effort because your broadcast is your baseline mood. Think of yourself like a radio station. Some of your emotional songs are uplifting and some are heavy. Whatever song is playing becomes a signal that you're transmitting into your environment.

If your baseline is gratitude, love, or confidence, that's what people feel. If your baseline is frustration, fear, or resentment, that's the station you're tuned into and that's what you're sending.

## The Diamond Mindset

A diamond understands this. Most people live from the outside in: when the world does this, I feel this. When they behave this way, I'm okay, but if they don't, I'm not. Their mood is controlled by external events and they become a victim to the environment.

*A diamond broadcasts from the inside out.* They're responsible for their mood. A diamond uses their boss identity to broadcast what you choose, what emotional signal that you choose to transmit.

You can fill your system with honey—love, courage, or patience—or you can flood it with poison—resentment and fear. Either way, you are broadcasting it. Your feelings in your body are transmitters. The feelings are energetic words to reality. Change your state, you change the signal that you send into your world.

And the ultimate frequency we're aiming for is *love*: creative, forward-moving energy. When you walk into a room and you're broadcasting love, you're not reacting to the environment, *you are creating it*. And that's what it means to become a diamond.

## Your Mission for Day 17

Today, let's keep it very simple:

**1. Choose one frequency word.** What do you want to transmit? It could be a word like calm, encouragement, respect, optimism, love.

**2. Take a slow, deep breath and say,** "This is the feeling that I'm activating in my body today. This is the signal that I'm transmitting."

**3. In three interactions today**—at work, home, or in the world—quietly ask yourself, "What am I broadcasting right now? Does it match the word that I chose or is it something different?"

If you drift, don't judge it. Just notice what came out of your transmitter. Use your tools to shift and come back to your chosen frequency. You're training yourself to broadcast like a diamond, *on purpose*, not by accident or default.
