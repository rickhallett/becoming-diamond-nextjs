## The Four Stages of Consciousness

There's a classic model of growth, often called the four stages of consciousness:

1. **Unconsciously unconscious** – You don't know that you don't know.
2. **Consciously unconscious** – You realize that you don't know.
3. **Consciously conscious** – You can do it, but you still have to think about it.
4. **Unconsciously conscious** – It becomes second nature. The new way of being is wired in.

Automatic deployment is that fourth stage. This is where your baseline has changed, so it's wired in. Your biological self is better prepared for future situations. Your tools and identities start to fire on their own.

## Training for Life

Think of characters like Jason Bourne or James Bond—highly trained individuals who respond in critical moments without having to think through each step.
