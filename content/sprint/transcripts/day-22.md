## The ACE Life Code

Today we start with the word *ACE* from the ACE Life Code in the book.

### A - Always

*A* stands for *always commit to embody*. I want to walk you through how I see each part of this.

*Always* is a strong word. It's called an absolute. For ACE, always means this: when I say yes to something that matters, I treat that yes as real. It's tied to *devotion*, the idea we talked about earlier on. You're not just flirting with change. You are *devoted* to it.

### C - Commit

Years ago I went to a seminar where I really saw how weak most commitments are. People say "I'm in" until it gets inconvenient. Then that commitment evaporates.

Now let me be clear: it's okay to decommit from things that are no longer aligned. Commitment is not a life sentence. But there's a huge difference between consciously decommitting and simply flaking out.

When you commit as a diamond, you're saying "As long as this is aligned, I'm in all the way." That's the level of commitment your diamond identity deserves.

### E - Embody

This is the key. *Embody* means you don't just think about your new identity. You *live* it. You embody your boss identity, your diamond identity, the version of you that is conscious, calm, and creative under pressure.

You're not just saying "I'm a diamond" as an affirmation. Your belief shows up in your body, your voice, your choices, your presence, and your frequency.

## Soul in a Body

I believe you are not a body with a soul. You are a *soul in a body*.

ACE is about letting that higher, wiser part of you—the boss, the diamond identity—run the show more often than your ego, your fear, or your old programming.

*Always commit to embody your highest identity in real moments with real people.* That's the ACE mindset. I'd love you to carry this long after the sprint ends.
