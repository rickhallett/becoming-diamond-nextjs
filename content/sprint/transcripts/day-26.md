## Welcome to Day 26

Congratulations on making it to day 26. Yesterday we explored flow, moving with life instead of fighting it. Today we arrive at the E in life code: *evolve*.

## Evolution is Change

Evolution is change. Anytime you evolve, you change. At any given moment, you are either growing or you're dying. And every time you grow, there's something you have to let go of to make room for what happens next.

Your ego does not like this. The ego craves certainty, sameness, and control. It wants the known. This is where your boss identity comes in and your diamond identity comes in.

## The Apex Optimist

A diamond uses every experience to their advantage. That's what I mean by the phrase *apex optimist*. An apex optimist is a person who can use any situation to grow, to get better, to move closer to who they want to be.

Today your job is to see yourself as *evolution in motion*. Every situation—good, bad, or neutral—is an opportunity for you to grow. Grow your patience, grow your courage, grow your emotional strength, grow your awareness.

## Evolving Your Biological Self

When you evolve your biological self, you're evolving your nervous system, your habits, your reactions, and you're feeding your consciousness and your soul. My belief is that every bit of wisdom that you squeeze out of life comes with you to whatever happens next.

Now you don't have to share that belief, but maybe you can entertain this idea: *Every experience I grow from is never wasted.*

## The LIFE Code

Evolution is the name of the game. And life gives me that path.

**L** for love. Love what life brings you, or at least accept it.

**I** - Integrate the lesson instead of letting it bounce off of you.

**F** - Flow with change instead of bracing against it.

And **E** - Evolve into a clearer, stronger, more conscious version of you.

The more you love, integrate and flow, the more you naturally evolve.

## Your Mission for Day 26

Today I want you to see everything as evolution practice. When something happens, especially something uncomfortable, ask:

- Is this here to evolve me?
- What is it asking me to grow?
- What part of me is ready to upgrade right now?

Maybe it's my patience. Maybe it's my communication. Maybe it's my boundaries. It could be my courage. Maybe my self-respect.

Choose one situation today and consciously say: *This is not a problem. This is evolution training. This is an evolution.* No more problems, just evolutions.

Then respond as the version of you who's growing and not shrinking.
