## Introducing the ART Protocol

Today we are going to go into the *ART Protocol*. Now ART is an acronym for *Accept, Release, Transform*. Acceptance is a skill, releasing is a skill, and transformation is a skill. And we're not going to turn a snowflake into a diamond without the transformation.

Awareness is the first key to change, and *acceptance is the second key to change*. A form of acceptance is when you say yes to something—you're accepting it.

## Understanding Acceptance

Now acceptance is a form of love. When you love somebody, you're accepting them. But when you resist them, you're not accepting them. So acceptance brings something closer and resistance pushes something away.

The saying is: *what you resist persists*. So if you resist being angry, you're going to keep it. If you resist depression, you're going to keep it. But when you accept it, you're bringing it closer and you're loving it. Now this is the courageous thing to do. This is the diamond thing to do.

## Love as the Universal Solvent

When you accept something and you love it, *love is the universal solvent*. So love solves, love dissolves. When you accept the anger as you feel it, simply by accepting it, you're releasing it. Emotion is energy in motion. So what you're doing is you're accepting the emotion that is already in motion, and you're allowing yourself to release the emotion.

And when you release it, you transform. And when you transform, you can step into your diamond identity. You can go into a different identity, whatever identity you want.

## Changing Your Baseline

If you don't change your baseline, you don't change. That's the way I look at it. But when you change your baseline, you change. And *when you change, everything changes*.
