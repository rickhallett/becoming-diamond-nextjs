## Introduction

Welcome to Day 11: Eliminating Energy Drains. Who's the boss? That's right, *you're* the boss. And as the boss, you need to know this: *where attention goes, energy flows*.

## Understanding Attention and Energy

Here's the spin. Imagine your attention is water and your energy is fertilizer. What happens if you water and fertilize a plant? It grows. And what happens if you water and fertilize a thought or belief? They too grow.

If your back is hurt and you keep obsessing over it, your attention and energy are stuck there. Not bad, but it creates divided attention. If you have to go to the bathroom while I'm training you, your attention is divided. Now add in, if you're worried about a family situation at home, your attention is divided again. *The more your attention is divided, the more energy drains you have.*

## Two Kinds of Energy Drains

There are two kinds of energy drains:

**Internal energy drains:** unprocessed emotions, resentments, worries.

**External energy drains:** clutter, unfinished projects, things you own that really own you.

In this sprint, as we grow your diamond identity, we want to let go of both. Your evening reflection and bookending are already helping you release internal drains so they don't stack and spill into your tomorrow. ART and ART² lets you let go in real time so you're not dragging the negative in this moment into your next moment.

## The Outer Reflects the Inner

Now, let's look outside. *Your outer world often reflects your inner world.* A chronically dirty car, a bedroom full of stuff you don't use, piles of clutter you keep stepping around—all of that is low-level stress and micro-weight on your consciousness.

I used to say this: *Do you own it, or does it own you?* A diamond keeps their consciousness light, not heavy. That means letting go of people—yes, people—places, and things that constantly drain you and don't help you go where you want to go.

## Today's Mission for Day 11

**One:** Internally, notice one inner energy drain—a resentment, worry, story—and use ART and ART² to release it and take responsibility for it in present time.

**Two:** Externally, choose one small area—a drawer, your desk, a corner of your car, your room—and clear it. Let go of something you don't need anymore.

As you release these drains, watch what happens. You will feel lighter, you will feel more conscious, and you will have more energy available for your diamond life.
