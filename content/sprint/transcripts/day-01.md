## Day One: Set Your Baseline

Today is about one thing: *awareness*. Before we try to fix anything, we need to see clearly how you already respond under pressure. Most people never do this. They just react, regret it, and move on. Diamonds pay attention.

### Your Diamond Move for Today

Your diamond move for today is very simple. I want you to *capture three moments* today when you feel any kind of stress or emotional charge. It could be a text, an email that annoys you, a customer, a client, a family member who triggers you, or even traffic, money, or technology acting up.

When it happens, just make a quick note on your phone, in your notebook, wherever. For each moment, write down three things:

1. **What happened?** Just the facts.
2. **What did you feel?** Name the emotion as best you can. Anger, fear, frustration, anxiety.
3. **How did you react?** Did you react like a snowflake—fragile, defensive, checked out? Or did you act like a diamond—present, grounded, responsive?

That's it. Three real-life moments captured honestly.

### Tonight's Reflection

Tonight, take one minute to read your notes and ask yourself one powerful question: *If I were already living as my diamond self, how would I handle these same situations tomorrow?*

You don't have to solve them all today. We're just shining a light on your current baseline so we can start to upgrade it. Awareness is the first cut that shapes the diamond.
