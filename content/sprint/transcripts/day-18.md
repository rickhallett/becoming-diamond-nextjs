## Introduction to Proximity Prime

Welcome to Day 18: Proximity Prime. Yesterday on Day 17 we talked about broadcasting, the idea that you're always transmitting a signal. Today we focus on where that signal lands. What happens to the room just because you're in it?

## The Thermostat vs. The Thermometer

As a corporate trainer, my job is to always have the highest energy of the room. I'm also responsible for maintaining that energy. I have to be *the thermostat and not the thermometer*.

I learned in a seminar a ritual that I still use today. When you arrive at the training venue, you walk the entire room. You touch every wall, you touch every table, every chair, and you feel into the space. What I'm doing is sending a powerful message to my nervous system that *I own this room, I own the space, and I am responsible for what happens here*. Anytime I can get to a venue early, I still do this today. It primes me to lead.

## The Rorschach Example

There's a movie called *The Watchmen* and a character named Rorschach in a prison cafeteria. He walks into it and a prisoner tries to take him out in a violent manner, and Rorschach takes him out in a violent manner. The guards grab him and he says to everybody in the cafeteria, "I'm not in here with you, you're in here with me."

That is *Proximity Prime*. He's saying my frequency is stronger than the collective frequency of this room.

## Your Boss Identity and Diamond Identity

That's what I want for you with your boss identity and your diamond identity. You give yourself permission to have the highest, most conscious, most coherent frequency in any room that you walk into. You're not doing it from ego or superiority, you're doing it from *responsibility*.

When I'm here, I bring clarity, I bring calm, I bring courage, and I bring love. That's Proximity Prime. Your presence is priming your nervous system to everyone around you.

## Your Mission for Day 18

Today, choose one room or situation where you'll practice Proximity Prime. It could be a meeting, your workplace, your living room tonight, at the gym, or even with a group of friends.

Before you walk in, use your Swiss Army Knife for 10 seconds. Stand tall. Breathe once or twice. Focus your mind. Silently say something like, *"I'm not in here with you, you're in here with me."*

Then simply notice: How do you carry yourself? How do people respond? Does the room feel any differently because you chose to own it?

You don't have to perform. You don't have to be loud. You just have to *own your presence and take responsibility for the field around you*. That's Proximity Prime.
