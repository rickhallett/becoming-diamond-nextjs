Congratulations on making it to Day 24: Integration Begins. We're in Week 4, *Shine*, and this week is about living and letting the Diamond Operating System really install.

## The Power of Integration

Today's idea is simple: *Integration is where the change actually sticks*. And the more you integrate, the more wisdom you gain.

A lot of people don't integrate with life's experiences. They hit them and bounce off of them, and they keep repeating the same patterns over. Diamonds are different. When you integrate with life, you actually receive the wisdom inside of your experiences—for your body, for your mind, and for your soul.

## Integrating with the Body

When we integrate with the body, we want to put our attention to where we feel it in our body. Now we're putting our consciousness in that part of our body. And what we're actually doing is changing the circuitry in your body and the way it transmits. You're accepting it at the same time, and you're also having the ability to ground yourself into the present moment.

## Integrating with the Mind

When we integrate with the mind, you want to take advantage of the situation from the mental perspective. You want to be as present as you can and say, "What can I learn from this?" You want to have *curiosity and enthusiasm*. The more curiosity you have and the more enthusiasm you have, the more of a diamond you are, because the integration increases with the curiosity and enthusiasm.

You don't want to look at it from one survival perspective. You want to look at it from multiple perspectives. *The more perspectives you look at things, the more integration you get. The more integration you get, the more wisdom you get.*

## Integrating with Your Soul

And then you want to integrate for your soul. You incarnated into this life to collect these experiences. So we want to take as many experiences as we can and integrate them as fully as we can so that we can take them to that eternal place. That is the name of the game.

And diamonds are conscious human beings. And as I've said over and over, *with consciousness comes capabilities*. So as you integrate deeper, you're going to become more conscious and you are going to have more capabilities to help you in the world today.

## Your Mission for Day 24

Choose one experience—it could be recent or current—that has some emotional charge.

**Integrate with the body:** Just sit for a moment. Feel where you feel it. Put your attention and consciousness in there. And breathe and let yourself feel it without running.

**Integrate with your mind:** Ask, "What is this trying to teach me? Is there another way I could look at this?"

**And then integrate in your soul:** Imagine that you're taking the wisdom from this experience and placing it in your heart, that part of you that continues to grow. A simple internal line could be, "Thank you for the wisdom. I'll use it."

And that's integration. You're not just collecting ideas, but what you're doing is you're letting life change you for the better.
