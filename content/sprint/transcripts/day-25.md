## What Is Flow?

Flow is how you move through life effortlessly, efficiently, and effectively.

## A Practice in Real Time

The other day I was at the airport at TSA. Now this is the line where you get status so you can move through the line quicker. There are six or seven lines open and of course I picked what I thought was the shortest one, and of course it turned out to be the longest one. At that moment I decided, "Okay, this is an exercise, this is my practice. I am going to stay present and I'm going to stay in the flow."

I watched my mind, I stayed in my body, paid attention to my breath, and *accepted the situation instead of fighting it*.

When I finally got to the front of the line, the TSA agent said it wasn't printed on my ticket. He said, "You can either go back to the ticket counter and get re-ticketed or you can go to that long line over there." And I said, "Okay, another choice point." Again I thought, "This is practice. I can either resist it or I can flow."

So what do you think I chose? I chose to stay in the flow. I accepted it, went to the regular line, and everything flowed perfectly. I got through the line, no drama.

## The Real Win

The real win wasn't the line. The real win was *not letting myself get twisted inside*. I stayed out of resistance, accepted it, and kept me in the flow.

And that's what happens in life. When you get twisted up inside, you're resisting something. But when you're in flow, you're in a *high state of acceptance*. When you're conscious, you move through life instead of against it. You become part of the solution and not part of the problem. That's flow.

## Your Mission for Day 25

Today your practice is simple. Use two words as your flow mantras: *yes* and *perfect*.

I oftentimes say "perfect" is the perfect word when it relates to your past. When something happens, big or small, notice your first reaction. Instead of tightening up and resisting, say inside or outside: *"yes"* or say *"perfect."*

You're not saying you like everything. You're training your system to accept the present situation—feeling the feeling, moving with it instead of against it.

If a situation tempts you into resistance, keep saying "yes, this is here" or "perfect, I can grow with this." That's flow. Not collapsing, not fighting, just moving with life in a way that keeps you light, adaptable, and creative.
