## What It Means to Kick Ass in the Sacred Game of Life

Welcome to Day 13: Presence as Communication.

I often talk about kicking ass in the sacred game of life. What does that even mean? For me, it means two things. First, you have fun and you honor the sacredness of life. Second, I reach the end of my life with minimal regrets. And I know two things that I would deeply regret: not being fully present with the people that I love and not playing the game full on. And if you're not present, you're not in the game.

Most people spend their time in the past or the future. They're regretting, rehearsing, and worrying. But the only place you truly exist at the most real level is *right now*.

## Moving Toward Diamond Clarity

Everything we've been doing in this sprint—your Swiss Army knife with the three blades (the body, the breath, the brain), art and ArtSquared protocols, the evening reflection, and letting go of stacked emotions—has been moving you towards *diamond clarity* and your new *diamond identity*. Why? Because a true diamond is present.

And when you're present, your awareness and consciousness goes up. And with consciousness comes capabilities. You become hyper-aware instead of stuck in survival mode. In survival, the brain defaults to the negative and fixates on what's wrong. In presence, you can see clearly, respond wisely, and you feel more alive.

## Presence as Communication

Remember, everything is interconnected, at the very least through air and space. When you're present, you're not just thinking—you're *communicating with everything around you*. That's why we say *presence as communication*.

Think about it. Have you ever tried to talk to someone who clearly wasn't present with you? How did it make you feel inside? Have you ever been with somebody who was totally present with you? Their eyes, their body, their energy, right there with you. How did that make you feel inside?

*Presence is felt before a single word is spoken.* When you're present and you have a thought, that thought is stronger. Your attention is undivided. When you're scattered in the past and future, your thoughts are divided.

## Your Uniquely Human Advantage

This is also one of the big differences between you and artificial intelligence, at least right now. AI can process data, mimic emotions, and respond fast, but it doesn't have your live, embodied human presence. It doesn't walk into a room and change the field the way that you can. *Your presence is part of what will keep you valuable and uniquely human in an AI-driven world.*

## Your Practice for Today

Today, your job is to practice presence using your Swiss Army knife:

**Blade One: Body.** Feel your feet on the ground, notice your posture, and let your body anchor you in this moment.

**Blade Two: Breath.** Bring your attention to your breathing. A few slow, conscious breaths pulls you back into the now.

**Blade Three: Brain.** Watch your thoughts. Notice when your ego runs off into the past or future and gently bring your focus back to your body and breath. This trains you to have *focused attention* instead of scattered, divided attention. Remember from Day 11, divided attention is weaker. Undivided, focused attention is incredibly powerful.

So today, wherever you are—on a call, in a conversation, walking, eating—practice: *Can I be here for this moment right now?*
