# The ART Protocol

You can't be present if you're not in your body.

{{video:7fbc888e-8fd6-4799-bbd9-2ff58b6ee806}}

You'll learn the ART Protocol: Accept, Release, Transform. Acceptance is the second key to change after awareness. What you resist persists, but when you accept difficult emotions, love acts as the universal solvent that dissolves them.

Your practice today is simple: when you feel triggered, ground yourself in your body and say "yes" to the emotion without labeling it. Feel it fully and let love dissolve it. This releases the energy and allows transformation.

When you change your baseline through this practice, everything changes. You'll step into your diamond identity through conscious acceptance and release.
