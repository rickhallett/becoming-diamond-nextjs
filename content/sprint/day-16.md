# Reality Reflects

The more you own, the more power you access.

{{video:5e5ee607-4b01-47c0-a733-1d373dc2dbd9}}

You'll explore how reality reflects your inner state through the coffee cup metaphor: when life "bumps" you, what spills out reveals what was already inside. Your reactions to triggers—anger, patience, anxiety, or presence—are clues to your baseline state.

Your mission is to notice three "bumps" today and ask yourself: "What just came out of my cup?" Use this awareness without judgment, then apply ART (Accept, Release, Transform) and your tools (body, breath, brain) to shift your state.

You're building awareness of the link between your inner world and outer reactions, gaining power to change what you carry and how you experience reality.
