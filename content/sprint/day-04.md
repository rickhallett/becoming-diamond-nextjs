# The Third Blade: Run The Brain

Your brain is the third blade. And it's not always your friend.

{{video:3749d405-889e-4ddf-b993-025f8969ef7e}}

You'll learn to control your nervous system through intentional breathing. When pressure hits, shallow breathing signals danger to your brain—but purposeful breathing flips that signal, telling your body you're safe and in control.

You'll master two powerful tools: Box Breathing (inhale-hold-exhale-hold for 4 counts each) to stay calm before high-pressure moments, and Heart-Focused Breathing (slow breaths while focusing on your heart) to reduce emotional reactivity and regain clarity.

Your mission: use box breathing before at least one pressure situation, and heart-focused breathing when emotions run high. Remember—when you control your breath, you control your state and your life.
