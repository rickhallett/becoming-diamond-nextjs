# Proximity Prime

Calm is magnetic. Focus is rare. You become both.

{{video:cf757872-ccec-40cd-a4b3-12affecc2e84}}

You'll discover **Proximity Prime**: owning the frequency of any room you enter from responsibility, not ego. Be the thermostat setting the temperature, not the thermometer just reading it. Learn the power of claiming space early—touching walls and furniture to tell your nervous system "I own this room."

Your mission: choose one situation today to practice this. Before entering, use your Swiss Army Knife for 10 seconds—stand tall, breathe, and silently affirm *"I'm not in here with you, you're in here with me."*

You don't need to perform or be loud. Simply own your presence and take responsibility for the field around you.
