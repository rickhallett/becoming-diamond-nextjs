# The Reflection

You don't need to find yourself. You write yourself.

{{video:2b258a9f-be8d-4be3-92b3-66af02d1bbec}}

You'll learn to master your life by bookending your day during alpha states (morning and evening). Each morning, write your diamond identities to prime yourself. Each evening, release negativity before it accumulates into tomorrow.

The evening reflection practice teaches you to say "yes" to negative feelings—acceptance dissolves them because love is the universal solvent. Ground yourself by feeling where emotions live in your body, then shift focus to approval.

End each day by asking "What did I do right today?" multiple times. This powerful habit rewires your focus toward progress. Set a reminder tonight to begin this bookending practice and unlock the transformation power of daily integration.
