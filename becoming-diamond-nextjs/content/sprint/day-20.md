# Hold Your Frequency

A Diamond can handle what glass would shatter under.

{{video:bcbb1e60-e622-43c2-b20c-93f34a553a93}}
You'll learn to hold your frequency even when others around you are struggling. Don't take their negativity personally or drop to their level—your job as a diamond is to stay steady, not fix or fight them.

You'll discover how emotional contagion works: stay conscious so your energy lifts others up rather than letting theirs pull you down. Use the mantra "I look for the perfection in the seeming imperfection" to stay present and kind without collapsing your energy.

Your mission: identify one person or situation that typically drags you down, notice the pull, take a conscious breath, and practice holding your frequency while giving them permission to be where they are.
