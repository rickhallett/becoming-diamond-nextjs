## Welcome to Day 8: Claiming Your Diamond Identity

Welcome to Week 2 Shift, Day 8: Claiming Your Diamond Identity.

Years ago, I learned from a prolific author named Brian Tracy: every day, write down who and what you want to be as a human being. And I proved out that principle. For at least 10 years in a row, I would go and buy the nicest journals I can find, and I would take the time every morning and write down what I wanted to be as a human being.

*I work harder on myself than anybody else could possibly imagine. I always go the extra mile. I am completely responsible for my life. I am a master of alchemy. I'm a master of acceptance. I'm a master of my emotions. I am the universe's most effective.*

I would write down these statements every day, and it would remind me, and I'm creating them.

## You Become What You Believe
