## Introduction

Welcome to Day 19: Elevate the Energy of the Room. We're still in week three, *Strengthen*. You've learned that you're always broadcasting. You've practiced the proximity prime. Today, the idea is simple: *don't just hold the room, lift the room*.

## The Challenge of Low Energy

As a trainer, the most challenging time to work with an audience is right after they've eaten a meal. They're digesting food, their energy is low, and their brains are full. And my job in that moment is to lift them up.

One of the ways I do this is with an exercise I call *the flash*. Everybody stands up and they run in place like the TV character. It looks goofy, but it gets the emotions moving. Now remember, *emotion is in the motion*. Sometimes I follow it up by getting them talking about badass moments—times in their lives where they felt proud and powerful. As soon as they move and talk about something that lights them up, the energy of the entire room rises.

## Leading by Example

Now you won't always be the corporate trainer or the person with the title, but I like to say: *in the absence of a leader, you are the leader*, and diamonds are leaders. There's leading by title, which we see way too much of, and there's leading by example, which we don't see enough of. Today is about leading by example.

## Plus Them

One simple way to elevate the energy is what I call *plus them*. Whenever you meet another human being, they're either feeling better or worse after they see you. Your job as a diamond is to plus them. Give them a real compliment. Notice something that they're doing right. Comment on something positive they choose—maybe their shoes, their effort, their attitude, a win they had. You're not flattering, you're lifting.

## Your Mission for Day 19

Today in one room or setting—work, home, or meeting—practice elevating the energy on purpose, with intention.

1. If you can get people moving: a quick stretch, a stand up, a little motion.
2. Plus three people: give three genuine compliments or acknowledgements.

That's it. *Move them, plus them*.
