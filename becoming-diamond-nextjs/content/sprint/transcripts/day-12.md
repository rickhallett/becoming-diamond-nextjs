Welcome to Day 12: Conscious Choices.

## The Power of Decision

Years ago, I was buying a house in Southern Oregon. I'd been looking at a bunch of places, overthinking every option. The moment I finally said "that's the one, I'm buying this house," I felt this huge relief of energy. That's the *power of decision*.

Before the decision, all my attention was scattered. This house or that house, this price or that price, this neighborhood or that neighborhood. The second I decided, all that mental noise collapsed into *clarity*.

## Two Places to Make Decisions

We can make decisions from one of two places: you can make it from your head or you can make it from your heart.

From your head, you're going to use logic, list pros and cons, or consider other people's opinions. Or from your heart—that deeper knowing that you just feel, that *this feels right*.

There's nothing wrong with using your head. You can absolutely write out pros and cons, ask trusted people, look at the data. But ultimately, the most powerful place to make a decision from is when you check in with your heart.

## Heart Intelligence

Back in Day 5, we talked about heart coherence and heart-focused breathing. Your heart is sending far more information up to your brain than your brain is sending down to your heart. Your heart is not just a pump—*it's an intelligence*.

Your boss identity, your *diamond self*, lives in your heart. Your ego identity mostly lives in your head, and it's often driven by survival, fear, pride, or the need for certainty.

When you live on autopilot, you repeat the same choices over and over because your ego wants safety and sameness. When you make a *conscious choice* from your heart, you're willing to step out of your comfort zone, relax into the unknown, lean into the unknown, and open the door to new experiences for you and your family.

Every real change in your life begins with a conscious decision.

## Your Homework for Day 12

**One:** Identify three choices you need to make in your life right now, big or small.

**Two:** Think about each one with your head—the facts, the pros, the cons.

**Three:** Do a minute of heart-focused breathing for each choice and feel what your heart says. Notice the difference.

**Four:** Choose one of those decisions and take action on it today.

When you act, you'll feel that same liberation I felt when I chose the house—that lightness, that sense of "okay, now we're moving."

And once you make that choice, don't torture yourself by going backwards and saying "what if I would have made the other choice?" *Own it*. There is no perfect path, only the path you commit to and grow through.
