## Welcome to Day 23

Congratulations on making it to Day 23 of the Life Code, LOVE. Yesterday on Day 22, we talked about ACE, Always Committing to Embodying Your Highest Identity. Today, we begin the Life Code. LIFE is an acronym for *Love, Integrate, Flow, and Evolve*. And over the next few days, we're going to go into each part. But today we're focusing on the L, which stands for, you got it, *LOVE*.

## What is LOVE?

For me, LOVE, at the highest level, is *the creative energy in the universe*. And what is the opposite of creation? If you said destruction, we're on the same page. You might even say yin-yang. When you're identifying as a diamond, you are identifying as a *creator* and not a destroyer.

Some of the aspects of LOVE are acceptance instead of resistance, connection instead of separation, being trustworthy versus not being trustworthy. LOVE has also been called *the universal solvent*. I learned this from Lester Levinson. LOVE solves and LOVE dissolves. LOVE also creates harmony in your body, and your body creates harmony in the world.

## Connection vs. Separation

Remember, LOVE is connection, and what is the opposite of connection? Yes, that's separation, or you might even say resistance. So when you resist life, you say, "I hate this. I don't want this. This shouldn't be happening to me." What you're doing is you're cutting yourself off from the wisdom that's inside of that moment.

LOVE says, "I may not like this, but I'm willing to face it and work with it." LOVE doesn't mean that you have to agree with everything. It doesn't mean that you have to let people walk all over you. *LOVE means that you stop warring with reality long enough to learn from it.*

## LOVE Starts With You

And as a diamond, LOVE starts with how you relate with yourself. Can I love this version of myself that's learning? Can I love the part of me that's still scared? Can I love the part of me that's still triggered and keeps moving forward anyway? And the answer is yes, yes, and yes.

The more you bring LOVE, real acceptance, and real connection to your life, the more energy you'll have, the more data you'll have, and the more you have to work with. That's why *LOVE is the first step in the Life Code*.

## Your Mission for Day 23

Your mission for Day 23 is to *practice LOVE*. Today, I want you to try this in a real situation. Think of one challenging area in your life. It could be a person, a habit, it could be a money situation, a health issue, or even something from your past.

And just think, just for one day, instead of attacking it or resisting it, try this: Quietly say to yourself, "I'm willing to bring a little more love to this. I'm willing to see it without so much resistance."

You don't have to feel blissful. You don't have to pretend that you like it. Just soften it just a little bit. Breathe in and see if you can hold the situation with a little bit less judgment and a little more acceptance. Notice what shifts in your body, in your emotions, and in your thoughts.

*That's LOVE as a living practice. It's not just a word.*
