## The Power of Micro-Interactions

Years ago, I met Phil Stutz, the author of the book *The Tools*. I teach *The Person With The Most Energy Wins*, and Phil teaches *The Person With The Most Micro-Interactions Wins*.

What is a micro-interaction? A sincere hello, a moment of real presence, a random act of kindness, giving just to give.

The law of reciprocity says that when you give, the universe finds a way to give it back to you. But the real power is when you *give with zero expectation of return*. That kind of giving shifts you, it lifts them, and it changes the field for both of you.

## Exponential Growth Through Small Actions

Think about exponential growth. If you start with one penny and double it every day for 31 days, you don't just end up with a few million—you end up with almost $11 million. And that's the power of small things done consistently.

*You are like that penny.* One kind word, one moment of presence, one micro-interaction can ripple into families, teams, and communities you will never see. We are a mimicking species, and people tend to imitate what was done to them unconsciously.
