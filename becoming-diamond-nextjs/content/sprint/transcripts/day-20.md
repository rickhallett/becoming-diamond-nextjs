## Welcome to Day 20: Hold Your Frequency

Today's lesson is this: *a diamond holds their frequency even when someone else is in a bad place.* Do you know anybody in a bad place? It could be at your workplace, your family, or anywhere else in your life.

In almost every class I teach, there's at least one person who looks miserable. They might be checked out, they could be in pain, or even openly negative.

## Don't Take It Personally

The first thing to understand is *don't take it personally*. Their face, their mood, their pain says more about them than it does about you.

The second thing is *don't drop down to their level*. You have people counting on you to stay in a higher state. As a diamond, your job is not to fight them nor fix them. Your job is to give them respect, give them fairness, and still hold your own frequency up.

## How to Engage

Sometimes I'll gently engage and I ask a question that will serve them, such as, "What would you like to get out of today?" And I do my best to give them that. I give them permission to be themselves without saying that. At the same time, I give myself permission to love my life and love what I'm doing.

## Lovers and Haters

In life, there are lovers and haters. Who do you think there's more of right now, lovers or haters? Most audiences tell me they believe there's more haters. There's more pain, more separation, and more polarization. As a diamond, you have to get good with that. *Don't let one person's misery become the story of your life.*

## Understanding Emotional Contagion

This is where emotional contagion comes in. If you're not conscious, their state will pull you down to theirs. But if you are conscious, your state will lift them up, even if it's just a little bit.

So your practice as a diamond is *love your life in spite of the person next to you who's hating theirs*. That's not always easy, especially if that person is your partner, your kid, or somebody close to you. But the principle is the same. Give them permission to be where they're at and don't take it personally.

## Your Swiss Army Knife

Use your Swiss Army knife: body, breath, and brain. ART: accept it, release it, and transform it. Stay coherent and stay steady. You are saying without words, *"I look for the perfection in the seeming imperfection."* What you're doing is looking for the best in them instead of the worst.

Sometimes when you do this, their baseline will shift up, even if it's just a little bit. Occasionally, it will shift up a lot. Oftentimes, they'll have a full turnaround. More importantly, you want to stay diamond.

## Your Mission for Day 20

Today, think of one person or situation in your life that tends to pull you down. When you're around them or it, notice the pull, take one conscious breath, and silently say, *"I look for the perfection in the seeming imperfection."* You do this and do your best to stay kind, present, and steady without collapsing your energy to match theirs. That's what it means to hold your frequency.
