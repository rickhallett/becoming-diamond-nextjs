## Master Your Life by Mastering Your Day

Welcome to Day 9: Reflection. If you want to master your life, simply master your day, because your life is built on days.

When we wake up in the morning, we're in a hypnotic state called an *alpha state*. When we go to bed, we're in a hypnotic state called an *alpha state*. What we want to do is *bookend our day*.

## The Morning Bookend

We want to start our day by priming ourselves with good information. That's where you take out your journal and you write down your *diamond identities* every morning, because that's going to remind you of who you're going to be that day.

## The Evening Bookend: Letting Go of Negativity

In the evening, when we reflect, we want to learn to let go of any negative stuff. If you have something negative that happens to you today, and if you don't let go of it today, guess where it goes? Into your tomorrow. And then if you have something else that happens the next day that's negative, and you don't let that go, guess where it goes? Into your next day. So we stack negative experiences on top of each other and we're dragging them forward.

That's why we want to create a ritual or routine of bookending our day. And at the nighttime bookend, we want to learn to let go of any negative things.

## My Evening Practice

I do this every evening. I shut off the TV early. I put my phone down. And what I'll do is I'll let myself use art and I'll accept. First, I'll be aware of where do I have any resentments? Where do I have any resistance? And then I'll let myself feel it. And I'll say, "Can I say yes to the anger?" Or, "Can I say yes to the fear?" Or, "Can I say yes to disapproving of the situation?" And I'll let myself do this for five, ten, sometimes fifteen, twenty minutes until I feel that it's cleared out.

Because remember, *yes is acceptance* and you're moving it closer to you. So when you're moving something closer to you, you're loving it, and *love is the solvent*—love dissolves. Always remember that *love is the universal solvent* and love dissolves. And acceptance is a form of love, and yes is acceptance.

So when I say yes to it, I'm accepting it. Or when I'm approving it, I'm accepting it. So when I say, "Can I say yes to disapproving of it? Can I allow myself to feel what I feel?" And I allow myself to pay attention to where I feel it in my body, like I'm storing it in my biological machinery.

And so now I'm putting—remember the *three blades, the Swiss Army knife*: the body, breath, and brain—now I'm putting my attention on my body and that grounds me. And I'm letting myself feel the feeling and I dissolve it, dissolve it, dissolve it.

And then I say, "Can I give this person, place, or thing a little bit of approval?" Yeah, can I give them a little bit more approval? Now I'm stacking positive on it. So now I'm going to bed without being a hater.

## Focus on What You Did Right

And then what I want to do is I want to focus on: *What did I do right today? What else did I do right today? What else did I do right today? What else did I do right today?*

This is one of the most powerful habits you can have before you go to bed. Say, "What did I do right today? What else did I do right today? What else did I do right today?" Because this gets you focusing on what you're doing right.
