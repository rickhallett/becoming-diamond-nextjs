## Welcome to Week Three

Congratulations on making it to week three strengthened and day 15: *Aligned Under Pressure*.

In week one, you stabilized your system. In week two, you shifted into your *diamond identity*. Week three is about one thing: Can you stay in that identity when there's pressure?

A snowflake melts under pressure, and a diamond is formed by the pressure.

## The Pressure-Reducing Valve

Years ago, I was a foreman on a big hospital job. The pressure from above was intense. That day, I made a decision: I am going to be the *pressure-reducing valve*. I feel the pressure, but my crew will not.

And that's what *aligned under pressure* looks like. You still feel the intensity, but you stay composed. You respond from your *diamond identity* instead of from panic, anger, or blame.

## Today's Practice

Today, I want you to keep it very simple. At some point today, life is going to squeeze you at least a little bit. It could be a conversation, a deadline, a text, money, family—something will happen.

In that moment, I want you to catch yourself and silently say: *"I am a diamond. I am the boss."*

Take one calm breath, feel your feet on the ground, and let your shoulders drop. Then just do your best to move through the moment without dumping your stress on everybody around you.

## Tonight's Reflection

Tonight, think back on that moment and ask yourself this: Did I stay more aligned than I usually do?

If the answer is even a little bit yes, that's a huge win. That's your *diamond identity* getting stronger.
