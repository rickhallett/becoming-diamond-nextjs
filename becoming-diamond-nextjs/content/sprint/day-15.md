# Alignment Under Pressure

Pressure isn't your enemy. It's your training ground.

{{video:a4ecb218-2775-4dd4-acfa-0e45caac62a7}}
You've reached week three—where you learn to maintain your diamond identity under pressure. Like a pressure-reducing valve, you'll feel the intensity but stay composed, responding from strength rather than panic or blame.

Today's practice is simple: when life squeezes you (a conversation, deadline, or stressor), silently say "I am a diamond. I am the boss." Take one calm breath, ground your feet, drop your shoulders, and move through the moment without dumping stress on others.

Tonight, reflect on whether you stayed more aligned than usual. Even small progress means your diamond identity is strengthening.
