# ACE Life

I'm not in here with you. You're in here with me.

{{video:23028f9d-5d90-401c-ac4a-590f4e20c65a}}
You'll learn the ACE Life Code: **Always Commit to Embody**. This means treating your commitments as real and devoted, not flaking when things get hard, and fully living your diamond identity through your body, voice, and choices—not just thinking about it.

Week 4 is about shining everything you've learned in real life. You're a soul in a body, and ACE helps your highest self lead instead of ego or fear.

**Today's mission**: Pick one situation where you normally slip into old patterns. Before entering it, remind yourself "ACE—I commit to embody my diamond self" and show up as that version of you.
