# Integration Begins

Are you broadcasting your truth — or reacting to someone else's frequency?

{{video:136b57be-2891-4f6c-93e3-c305060030b4}}

You'll discover that integration is where real change sticks. Instead of bouncing off experiences and repeating patterns, you'll learn to integrate on three levels: body (feeling where experiences live physically), mind (asking what you can learn with curiosity and multiple perspectives), and soul (placing wisdom in your heart as something eternal).

Your mission is to choose one emotionally charged experience and integrate it fully. Feel it in your body without running, ask what it's teaching you, then thank it for the wisdom. This process transforms life experiences into lasting capabilities and consciousness.
