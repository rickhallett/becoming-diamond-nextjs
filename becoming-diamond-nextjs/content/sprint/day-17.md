# Broadcasting Begins

Exemplary leadership doesn't need applause. It creates impact.

{{video:c349a5d3-c4a5-4a29-bdcf-028a6cc23ba0}}
You're always broadcasting an emotional frequency—your baseline mood becomes the signal you transmit to the world. Most people react to their environment (outside-in), but diamonds broadcast intentionally from within (inside-out), choosing to fill their system with love, courage, and confidence rather than fear and resentment.

Today you'll choose one frequency word (like calm, optimism, or love) and consciously activate it in your body. During three interactions, you'll notice what you're actually broadcasting and gently shift back to your chosen frequency when you drift.

You're learning to broadcast on purpose, not by default—creating your environment instead of reacting to it.
