# Eliminating Energy Drains

What if every trigger was just a teacher in disguise?

{{video:7d93e8c6-3772-4941-85d5-05190293340b}}

You'll discover how divided attention creates energy drains that deplete your power. Learn about two types of drains: internal (unprocessed emotions, resentments, worries) and external (clutter, unfinished projects, possessions that own you). Your outer world reflects your inner world.

You'll complete two clearing actions today: use ART and ART² to release one internal energy drain, then clear one small physical area and let go of something you don't need.

As you eliminate these drains, you'll feel lighter, more conscious, and have more energy available for your diamond life. Where attention goes, energy flows—so choose wisely.
