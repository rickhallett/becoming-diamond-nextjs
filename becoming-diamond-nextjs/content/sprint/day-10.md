# The Boss

Accept. Release. Transform.

{{video:a4fc1ef2-9ad2-4597-bd9d-ab5afcb96374}}
Day 10 introduces you to "The Boss"—your consciousness. You'll learn that while your autonomic nervous system automates breathing, heartbeat, and habits, you can control your body, breath, and brain through conscious awareness. This consciousness is your diamond identity.

You'll discover that discipline means doing what your ego resists, and that how you do anything is how you do everything unless you choose to change. With consciousness comes new capabilities.

Your key practice: Declare "I'm the boss" and consistently ask yourself whether you're acting from automation or consciousness. This boss identity empowers you to break automated patterns and achieve transformation you've never experienced before.
