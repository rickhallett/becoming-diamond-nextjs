# Frequency as Currency

You've changed more than you know.

{{video:a35dbcdc-268b-4e00-9abc-5f26216bdfe1}}

Day 21 explores frequency—your consciousness and state of being—as real-world currency. In an AI-driven world, you become more valuable through higher awareness, steady presence, and the ability to uplift others. These aren't soft skills; they're economic advantages that set you apart.

You'll evaluate yourself with one powerful question: If your frequency is your currency, how rich are you right now? Then identify one small upgrade you can make this week—choosing calm over reaction, gratitude over complaint, or using your tools before key conversations.
