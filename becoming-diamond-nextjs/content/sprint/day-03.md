# The Second Blade: Breathe Before You Break

Your breath is your remote control.

{{video:bb194ce7-00b2-45c1-a10f-969a630f8625}}

Day 3 teaches you to control your nervous system through intentional breathing. When pressure hits, shallow breathing signals danger to your brain—but breathing on purpose flips that signal, telling your body you're safe and in control.

You'll learn two powerful tools: Box Breathing (inhale-4, hold-4, exhale-4, hold-4) for staying calm before high-pressure moments, and Heart Focus Breathing (breathe slowly while focusing on your heart) for managing emotional triggers.

Your mission: use box breathing before one pressure situation and heart focus breathing when emotionally overwhelmed. You're training your system to respond like a diamond under pressure.
